# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Odysseus is for technical operators, builders, and power users running a self-hosted AI workspace. They move between chat, agents, documents, email, notes, calendar, research, plugins, and local model workflows, often in long sessions where speed, trust, and recoverability matter more than spectacle.

## Product Purpose

Odysseus gives one local workspace for AI-assisted work across private data and tools. Success means users can understand system state quickly, act with confidence, keep sensitive workflows scoped, and recover from mistakes without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Capable, quiet, and operator-grade. The product should feel like a serious cockpit for complex work: responsive, legible, dense when useful, and human enough to avoid feeling sterile.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS marketing language, purple-blue gradient monoculture, glassy demo-app surfaces, oversized hero treatments inside product screens, decorative card stacks, and designs that hide operational state behind ambiance. Avoid UI that feels cute before it feels reliable.

## Design Principles

1. Operational clarity first. Every screen should make current state, available actions, and consequences easy to scan.
2. Density with manners. Odysseus may show a lot at once, but hierarchy, spacing, and restrained labels must keep it readable.
3. Respect local control. Design should reinforce ownership, scoped access, confirmation gates, and recoverability.
4. Integrations should feel native. Plugin and agent surfaces should inherit Odysseus patterns instead of becoming disconnected mini-apps.
5. Calm over hype. Motion, copy, and color should reduce cognitive load, not advertise themselves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives, touch-safe controls on mobile, and text that survives long labels, translated strings, and dense operational data.
